<p><b>TU Expense Tracker</b> is a modern personal finance app designed for simplicity, privacy, and speed.</p>

<p>Instead of manually typing every daily expense or giving third-party apps access to your bank accounts, TU Expense Tracker <b>automatically reads your incoming bank transaction SMS alerts</b> directly on your device.</p>

<ul>
  <li><b>100% Private &amp; Offline First</b>: Everything stays strictly on your phone in a local SQLite database. No data ever leaves your device.</li>
  <li><b>Zero Manual Effort for Routine Purchases</b>: Categorize a store once (e.g., <i>Swiggy → Food</i>), and the app remembers it forever.</li>
  <li><b>Smart Multi-Category Splitting</b>: Easily split multi-item receipts across several categories.</li>
  <li><b>Beautiful Modern UI</b>: Built with Flutter featuring smooth rounded fintech cards, vibrant category emojis, customizable accent palettes, and a true battery-saving OLED pitch black mode.</li>
  <li><b>Automated Bank SMS Ingestion</b>: Reads transactional SMS from major banks and UPI gateways in real time without waking up unnecessary background services.</li>
  <li><b>Visual Spending Dashboard</b>: Interactive charts, trend analysis, and multi-month comparison.</li>
  <li><b>Fast Search</b>: Search notes and merchant names across your entire ledger with zero lag.</li>
  <li><b>1-Click Spreadsheet Backup</b>: Export your entire financial history to a standard .xlsx workbook viewable directly in Google Sheets or Excel.</li>
</ul>

<p>Take back control of your finances without sacrificing your privacy!</p>
